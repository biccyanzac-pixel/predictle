# Question formats

Every format scores 0–100 from how close the guess was. The curve is the same shape everywhere:

**score = 100 × exp(−(error / σ)²)**

| Format | You do | Error measured | σ | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Where does it stop?** | Tap where ball A comes to rest (drag to adjust; a magnifier appears under your finger on phones) | 2D distance | 18% of world width | 5% off → 93, 10% → 73, 20% → 29 |
| **Freeze frame** | Watch the start; it freezes just before the twist (the key moment), then tap where it will stop | 2D distance | 18% of width | easier: you've seen the ball's speed |
| **How high?** | Drag a line to the highest point ball A reaches after a spring, seesaw or bumper launches it | height | 15% of world height | 5% off → 89, 10% → 64 |
| **Domino count** | Pick how many dominoes fall (− / +) | count | max(1.6, 15% of the row) | exact 100, off by 1 → 68, by 2 → 21 |
| **Stopwatch** | Slide to how many seconds until ball A first touches the floor | time | 25% of the true time (min 0.4 s) | 10% off → 85, 25% → 37 |
| **Place the bumper** | Choose one of 6 spots for a bumper so ball A stops on the ★ | where your choice's ball stopped vs the star | 18% of width | the right spot = 100; a near miss still scores |

A day's total is the sum of its three scores, out of 300. Each puzzle also gets an emoji: 🟢 90+, 🟡 60+, 🟠 30+, 🔴 below 30.

All of these are pre-recorded. "Place the bumper" ships 6 recordings, one per spot, and your choice picks which one plays.

## Varying world size

Every puzzle is 3:4 portrait, so it always fits a phone. The scale varies per puzzle:
- **7.5 m wide:** big pieces, few of them
- **9 m wide**
- **10.5 m wide:** more, smaller pieces

Scores are relative to the world size, so a 0.5 m miss counts for more in a small world.

## More ideas, not built yet
- **Which first, A or B?** Two balls are released together. Pick which reaches the floor first, then slide by how much (scored on the time gap).
- **Tower: stand or fall?** Stack blocks, then a ball hits them. Predict how many blocks stay standing.
- **Blackout:** the scene plays, then goes dark mid-flight. Tap where the ball is when the lights come back on (after N seconds).
- **Ghost path:** draw the ball's path with your finger, scored on the average distance from the real path. Hard to make fair on phones.
- **Order the finish:** three balls with different materials, the same start and the same ramps. Drag them into finishing order (scored on correct pairs).
- **Bigger Sunday design puzzle:** choose a spot and an angle for a ramp (6 × 3 options, all pre-simulated).
